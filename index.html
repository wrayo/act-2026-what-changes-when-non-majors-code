Why Teach Social Science Majors to Code?

This course combines R programming with statistical reasoning

  • Data science skills are increasingly important in the labor market and academic research (Williams, 2022)
  • Opportunity to introduce students with less quantitative experience to the challenges of coding in a supportive environment.
  • Programming provides students with immediate feedback and frequent opportunities to solve problems, building confidence to help support persistance on the more difficult statistical reasoning.

How can we support students learning programming and statistical reasoning together?

Reading Bayes Factors

BF10 compares a model allowing change with a model fixing change at zero

Evidence Favors change Favors no change
Equal support 1 1
Anecdotal 1 – 3 1/3 – 1
Moderate 3 – 10 1/10 – 1/3
Strong 10 – 30 1/30 – 1/10
Very strong 30 – 100 1/100 – 1/30
Extreme > 100 < 1/100
  • BF01 = 1/BF10
  • Labels are conventions
  • Evidence strength differs from change size

Intervals show change size. Bayes factors show relative evidence.
